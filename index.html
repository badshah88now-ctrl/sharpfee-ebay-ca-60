eBay Fees in Canada: What a C$60 Order Really Costs

eBay is the biggest resale marketplace in Canada, and its fee structure is simple to state but easy to underestimate. On a C$60 sale, the final value fee alone takes C$8.16 — and that is before the per-order charge.

For most categories, eBay Canada charges a final value fee of 13.6% of the total sale. On C$60 that is C$8.16. On top of that, eBay adds a per-order fee of $0.40 for orders over $10. The total eBay cost on a C$60 order is therefore about C$8.56, leaving you with roughly C$51.44 before shipping and the cost of the item itself. That is more than 14% of the sale price.

The fee is charged on the total sale, so a higher shipping charge pushes the fee up too — worth remembering when you set postage. And because the final value fee is a percentage, it scales with price: a C$120 item costs twice as much in fees as a C$60 one, even though the work of listing it is the same.

Compare that with your own store. Shopify Canada Basic costs $49 CAD a month, and each order costs 2.8% plus 30 cents in card fees — about C$1.98 on a C$60 order. The gap between eBay's C$8.56 and Shopify's C$1.98 is C$6.58 per order, so your own store pays for itself once you pass roughly eight C$60 orders a month. Below that volume, eBay's enormous buyer base justifies the fee. Above it, you are handing eBay money for customers who would happily buy direct.

The smart move is to run the numbers before you list.
